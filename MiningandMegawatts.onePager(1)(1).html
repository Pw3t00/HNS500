The Energy Paradox

CAR holds immense geological wealth (Gold, Iron, Copper, Cobalt, Diamonds, Uranium) but remains stifled by a 16% electrification rate.

Industry relies on fragmented, expensive diesel generation. This creates a massive arbitrage opportunity. SmartEnergy Holdings replaces inefficient diesel with a centralized, reliable large Hybrid utility-scale power plant, cutting costs by ~60% while capturing healthy margins.

  • ⚠️ Pain Point: Diesel costs $0.35 - $0.60 per kWh.
  • ✅ Solution: HNS500 Target Tariff ~$0.15 per kWh.

🌍

MIGA Political Risk Insurance

Provided by the World Bank Group, MIGA insurance protects equity against non-commercial risks.

  • ✓ Expropriation Protection
  • ✓ War & Civil Disturbance
  • ✓ Transfer Restriction (Currency)
  • ✓ Breach of Contract
🏦

IDA Partial Risk Guarantee (PRG)

Backstops the government's PPA payment obligations.

  • ✓ Payment Security: If the offtaker fails to pay, IDA pays the commercial lenders.
  • ✓ Deterrence: Default to IDA triggers a cross-default on all World Bank aid, prioritizing SmartEnergy payments.
🤝

AfDB Partial Credit Guarantee

African Development Bank support enhances the credit profile of debt tranches.

  • ✓ Extends commercial loan tenors.
  • ✓ Provides liquidity facilities to ensure consistent cash flow.
